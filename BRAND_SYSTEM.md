### Absolute logo rule

Do **not** invent, regenerate, trace, approximate or reinterpret the missing AIPT logo variants. Do not use typography/CSS recreation as a substitute for an approved logo file. Do not adapt another business unit's asset.

## 5. Asset provenance requirements

Before a new AIPT-owned brand asset becomes canonical, record/verify:

1. AIPT ownership/approval provenance;
2. source file and intended surface;
3. light/dark usage;
4. minimum readable size and clearspace behavior;
5. whether the asset contains rasterized text or embedded metadata;
6. small/mobile/header/social QA;
7. rollout and rollback reference.

Provider logos remain provider assets and must not be presented as AIPT identity.

## 6. Usage rules

### Allowed now

- Keep the current live favicon and current live navbar/footer identity unchanged.
- Use `aipt-icon.svg` for a new icon-only AIPT surface when the existing production mark is appropriate.
- Use locked AIPT color/type/tone direction for normal product/UI work without pretending that a generated wordmark is approved.

### Blocked until approved wordmarks exist

- replacing navbar/footer wordmark construction under AIPT-0102;
- creating invoices/emails/partnership headers that require a full AIPT logo;
- creating a default AIPT social/OG brand card by inventing a missing wordmark;
- producing reversed/horizontal logo variants from approximation.

## 7. Clearspace and minimum-size policy

Exact logo clearspace/minimum-size geometry cannot be finalized until the approved wordmark source package exists. For the current icon:

- preserve the full square canvas and rounded-square silhouette;
- do not crop into the mark;
- do not stretch or alter aspect ratio;
- do not recolor individual white strokes;
- keep enough surrounding whitespace that the mark remains visually distinct.

Do not publish invented numeric clearspace rules as if they came from an approved brand manual.

## 8. Prohibited use

- another portfolio brand's logo under the AIPT name;
- provider logo used as AIPT seller identity;
- unsupported "official partner", "authorized reseller" or similar implications;
- AI-generated replacement logo;
- distorted, recolored or effects-heavy variants of the canonical icon;
- unsupported customer/rating/market-leadership claims embedded into brand artwork.

## 9. Rollout surfaces after approval

When the complete approved family is available, AIPT-0102 may roll it out deliberately to:

- navbar and mobile menu;
- footer;
- favicon/PWA manifest where explicitly approved;
- Open Graph/social cards;
- order confirmation, receipts/invoices;
- transactional/marketing email templates;
- Admin / future Control Center;
- product/media templates and watermark rules;
- video end cards;
- support/WhatsApp/business-profile graphics.

Every rollout must preserve layout stability, accessibility, current SEO behavior and production rollback capability.
