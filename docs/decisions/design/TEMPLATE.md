## Evidence

State the verified brand artifact, measured CRO data, accessibility defect, security issue, or compliance requirement. Link/identify the source and date. Preference-only rationale is not evidence.

## Hypothesis

State exactly what the proposed visual/system change should improve and why the evidence supports that expectation.

## Scope

List the routes/components/styles/assets that will change and what is explicitly out of scope.

## Acceptance

Define measurable pass/fail criteria and guardrails. Preserve checkout/order/search behavior unless the approved hypothesis explicitly changes them.

## Rollback

Record the rollback commit/reference and the conditions that trigger rollback.

## Decision notes

Before merge, set `Decision: PROCEED` only when the evidence, scope, acceptance and rollback are complete and the owner accepts the change.
